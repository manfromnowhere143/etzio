## The implemented step

`FileEvidenceStore` previously caught ordinary exceptions during shard and temporary-file
setup but missed `KeyboardInterrupt` and `SystemExit`. An acquired shard descriptor could
remain open; temporary setup could leave both a descriptor and an unpublished file.
The acquiring helper now cleans up before propagating the original interruption.

The `16` controls cross four setup sites, both write entrypoints and both interruption
classes. They fail on the predecessor and pass after correction on CPython `3.11.15` and
`3.14.2`; all `79` focused evidence tests pass. They also check existing bytes, absence of
new publication and temporary files, retry and idempotence. The full collection target is
`2645`; exact full-suite, package and CI results belong to the release record named in the
[handoff](SESSION_HANDOFF.md). This is bounded exception injection with successful cleanup,
not arbitrary signal delivery, process-kill recovery, power-loss qualification or a bounty.

The repair was selected from a reproduced local defect. It is not a reproduction of any
paper above or evidence that the engine discovers third-party vulnerabilities.
