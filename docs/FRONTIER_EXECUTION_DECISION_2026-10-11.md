## Verdict

The strongest product hypothesis is a system that turns an authorized candidate into
independently replayable evidence and a useful repair, with measurable reviewer savings.
The target-neutral kernel and versioned domain adapters support that hypothesis. The
present implementation does not yet test it on an external target. More rules, agent
roles, signatures or test cases do not establish commercial value by themselves.

The next foundation milestone remains **one explicitly admitted complete native-provider
lifecycle reproduced cold through all four retained phases**. Its external administration,
freshness, revocation and recovery requirements cannot be supplied by owned fixtures.
The prepared FreeTSA diagnostic is one finite information-gathering action toward native
material; even a successful response would leave most of that milestone open.

The authorized offline continuation here is an executable falsification of an evaluation
assumption, before it becomes part of structured execution evidence. It is deliberately a
finite research experiment, not an early implementation of CATO or a new target domain.

## Executed falsifier

The [experiment contract](EFFECT_EVIDENCE_EXPERIMENT.md) precedes its implementation.
All `16` permission policies and `256` transitions of an owned bit store were enumerated.
The rule “the effect disappeared after the patch” accepted `8/16` policies; `7/8` of
those fail the complete authored specification. Three break legitimate use, and four
leave a second unauthorized route. Only one policy satisfies both requirements.

Both supported Python runtimes produce identical reports. A separate permission-table
characterization agrees with every transition-based verdict. The controls also preserve
missing/incomplete observations and demonstrate two causes sharing an effect and patch.
This is not independent operational verification or an empirical false-positive rate on
real software. No production gate is added or declared closed.

**Design consequence:** a future receipt needs separate evidence for the effect, causal
attribution, repair, and preserved legitimate behavior. A signed hash of a report cannot
supply those meanings. A model can propose properties and patches; it cannot authorize
the property specification or independently verify its own candidate.

## What would count as substantial progress

| Claim | Present evidence | Next falsifier and stop rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native integrity works outside owned fixtures | Historical format and signature compatibility; no complete admitted roster | Cold lifecycle replay under independently evidenced administration. Refuse stale/missing/revoked inputs, substituted scope and delayed use. Stop claiming native finality until the full chain passes. |
| Recovery survives local loss | Protocol fixtures and documented unresolved boundaries | Lose local DB, cache and staging after `H1`; recover `H1` or block. A coherent `H0` restore must not become current. No signature-count proxy for independent custody. |
| Independent execution establishes an effect | Opaque modeled receipts only | After storage and isolation gates, replay an owned benign workload under separate builder/verifier identities. Altered target, forged output, missing state and surviving orphan work must refuse. |
| A repair preserves useful behavior | This finite counterexample only | Frozen legitimate-workflow checks alongside security probes. A disabled feature, failed startup or broken seed cannot count as a repair. |
| Discovery improves | Six static rule classes and two original fixtures; no comparative result | After the existing gates, equal-budget static, model-only and hybrid arms on frozen EVM holdouts. Retain all failures, contamination exposure and unsupported cases. No live-program recall without ground truth. |
| Customers benefit | No retained buyer or paid outcome | Under a separately authorized concrete engagement, measure independent reviewer minutes, accepted repairs and actual cost; count settled payment separately. Stop revenue forecasting without purchasing evidence. |

For the later comparison, freeze target families, information access, effective time and
cost caps, oracle version, eligible outcomes and failure treatment before running agents.
Count unique adjudicated causes, not reports or probe triggers. Pair methods on the same
targets; repeated seeds describe within-target variability rather than independent targets.
Preserve original scores when an oracle is corrected, issue a new version, and replay every
affected artifact. Set the minimum useful improvement with the real reviewer before the
evaluation; no universal percentage is justified by current evidence.

Do not promote an allocation, memory or learning change merely because it improves the
development set. Require untouched evaluation and measured benefit after its full compute
and review cost. Keep models replaceable. Expand domains only when the existing domain's
effect and legitimate-behavior oracles transfer under a new versioned contract.
