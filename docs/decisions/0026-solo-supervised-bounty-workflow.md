# ADR-0026: Solo supervised bounty workflow

- Status: operator-directed supervised workflow
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Owner: Daniel Wahnich
- Supersedes: ADR-0025's outside-review prerequisite and the containment preparation
  documents' mandatory independent-review step, for supervised work only

## Decision

Daniel challenged the requirement to recruit an outside reviewer when he and the
assistant operate this mission. After a concrete solo-workflow proposal, he
explicitly directed removing internal obstacles and continuing bounty research.
The assistant is responsible for advancing this workflow without another request
to appoint a third party. This changes an internal prerequisite, not the authority
granted by a target owner or the evidentiary meaning of independent verification.

The assistant may review and accept a concrete disposable isolated execution
profile from retained measurements, then build and exercise exact scoped local
targets under a separate recorded target/profile contract. A qualified environment
and target-specific authority remain necessary. Previous owned-fixture passes do
not automatically admit a larger guest, new toolchain or arbitrary build input.

The assistant may develop and repeat a candidate from clean retained inputs,
checking an explicit effect oracle and negative controls. Such evidence is
**same-assistant self-reproduction**. A different process, fresh machine, second
model or repeated run does not turn it into independent verification. A report
draft may state its measured result and this provenance without waiting for an
outside containment reviewer. Actual program triage or separate review remains
pending until an identifiable reviewer performs it.

## Concrete execution contract

Before each target build or proof attempt, retain:

1. The exact program terms, target revision, scope applicability, allowed actor
   and actions, and relevant known-issue and eligibility checks.
2. Compiler, dependency, input and runner identities; the isolated profile's
   measured bounds; denied execution egress and credentials; finite time, memory,
   output and resource budgets; cancellation and verified cleanup.
3. The proposed effect, expected legitimate behavior, contrary control, exact
   reproduction command and fresh-input repeat procedure. A toolchain calibration
   or source hypothesis must not be reported as a target vulnerability.

The scope must be concrete and reviewable. Do not substitute blanket instructions
to attack for permission from the target's program. Preserve unrelated systems,
private evidence and exact resource ownership. Existing bounded benign cloud
qualification remains authorized; any target dispatch must separately bind its
permitted actions and resource grant. No live-network action, credential use,
unbounded spending or disclosure follows from this document.

## Outcomes and reporting

Record source-only, failed reproduction, known issue, excluded impact,
self-reproduced candidate, independently reproduced, submitted, accepted and paid
as distinct outcomes. A self-reproduced result becomes a report draft only when
the demonstrated effect fits the program; duplicate clearance and eligibility
must be stated with their actual limits. Submission and disclosure still require
their separate scoped authority. Program acceptance and payment are external
outcomes, never inferred from local checks or advertised reward ceilings.

## Production separation and present state

Production kernel generator/verifier separation, native integrity providers,
external latest-head recovery, protected storage and comparative benchmarks retain
their existing requirements. This workflow cannot issue a production kernel
finding or promote modeled receipts into native evidence.

At adoption, no target execution profile or eligible report candidate is admitted.
The next practical step is a bounded isolated compiler/test-runner calibration,
then exact scoped target work. The retained Aave, GHO and CoW source dispositions
remain unchanged, and the separate Immutable session remains untouched.
