# ADR-0025: Supervised bounty research alongside engine qualification

- Status: operator-directed mission ordering; per-target execution remains gated
- Date: 2026-10-24
- Owner: Daniel Wahnich
- Supersedes: ADR-0001 only for the sequencing of supervised bounty research

October 25 update: [ADR-0026](0026-solo-supervised-bounty-workflow.md) supersedes
this decision's mandatory outside-review prerequisite for supervised work. The

## Decision and authority

On October 24 Daniel explicitly directed bounty research to proceed alongside the
continued engine build, with practical attempts and retained outcomes driving
improvement. He also assigned another session to the effort. This supersedes the
earlier decision to place every bounty attempt behind complete production engine
qualification. It does not establish any particular target, finding, income or
permission from a third-party owner.

Run two workstreams:

1. Continue native integrity, external latest-head recovery, protected storage,
   independent MARCELLUS/CATO and the registered engine comparison under their
   existing contracts. A pending governed transition remains pending.
2. Prepare and operate a supervised, private bounty-report workflow under exact
   program scope, a bounded target contract, an accepted isolated reproduction
   environment and independent review. The workflow must report its provenance
   and limitations directly; it cannot issue production kernel findings or claim
   that the engine's native integrity gates have passed.

Independent catalog and monitor arrangements remain engine dependencies. They are
not prerequisites for ordinary supervised source review or a separately accepted
isolated report reproduction. The registered comparative benchmark remains
necessary for an engine-performance claim; it is not a prerequisite for an
individually reproduced, independently reviewed report.

## Entry and reporting contract

Before executing a target or its build system, retain an exact target/revision and
applicable program terms, permitted actors/actions, reproduction method, input and
output handling, finite resource budget, cancellation and cleanup conditions, and
the accepted isolation measurements. Missing target identity or containment blocks
execution for that target. Public source and audit review can continue.

Only reproduced effects within the advertised impact scope become report
candidates. Check known issues, audits, patched versions and exclusions before
investing in a proof. Retain negative controls and ordinary legitimate behavior.
Another model's agreement or a second role name is not independent reproduction.
Record the actual verifier, separate execution, retained inputs and effect oracle.
Submission and disclosure retain their separate scoped authorization requirements.

For CoW, contract research must follow the program's local-replica/private-testnet
requirements. A public reward ceiling is not an estimate of income. The currently
retained historical source mapping does not establish current deployed code or
state, and Daniel's personal eligibility remains unconfirmed.

## Present state and next experiment

No target execution profile is admitted by this decision. The October 24 owned
KVM instruction experiment is benign host preparation under the existing October 9
cloud grant. Its corrected run passed seven benign controls and both attempts'
resources were verified absent; the [retained result](../BENIGN_KVM_PREFLIGHT.md)
does not qualify arbitrary code.

The next dependency-complete report experiment must bind one candidate, one exact
eligible target, one measured isolated environment and an independent reviewer.
The experiment may end with a known issue, excluded impact, failed reproduction,
inconclusive result or eligible report. Record time and cost for every outcome.

The prior zero-quantity fee-replay question is closed as a publicly documented CoW
known issue, not a new finding. Its official documentation is pinned at commit
`b5e4e68d6c2462f5e87143759ac30e354dd76538` and retained privately with byte identities.

## Concurrent work

Use separate Git worktrees and disjoint experiment resource names. Record target,
revision, candidate IDs, owned paths, verification role and result. A session must
not claim another session's result without inspecting its retained evidence.

The original engine mission and domain breadth remain intact. Supervised reports
can supply observed failure modes and reproducible cases for later engine
evaluation; they do not change authority policy or production bytes automatically.
