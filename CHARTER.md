# Etzio Charter

Etzio is a private research program intended to become an enterprise-grade system for
**authorized vulnerability research**. Its objective is to discover, reproduce, adjudicate,
and responsibly package real security defects while preserving an exact chain from
authority to evidence.

The mission is broader than a scanner, one language, or one bounty ecosystem. Etzio should
eventually support source, binary, protocol, cloud, web, mobile, smart-contract, supply-chain,
and configuration research through versioned adapters. It earns breadth by passing measured
gates; it does not weaken the kernel for a new category.

## Purpose

Convert an admitted target revision into a governed research mission whose findings are
falsifiable, independently reproduced, replayable, and bounded by the authorization that
made the work legal.

Bounty revenue is one useful external measure of economic value. It is neither the source of
authority nor the only product goal. Defensive discovery, enterprise assurance, benchmark
progress, negative results, and transferable research knowledge are also first-class.

## Operating laws

### 1. Authority precedes existence

The kernel admits an exact, valid, unexpired `TargetContract` before opening a mission.
Everything not named is denied. Repository possession, a URL, a model instruction, a
credential, prior access, or a program’s existence does not establish scope.

### 2. Consequential effects have separate grants

Read-only analysis, network recon, dynamic interaction, exploit execution, credential use,
paid compute, disclosure drafting, submission, and publication are distinct actions. One
grant never implies another. Timeout, retry exhaustion, or missing review never implies
approval.

### 3. The generator never confirms itself

The identity that proposes or constructs a candidate cannot issue its terminal verdict.
CATO operates through a separate execution identity, isolation boundary, evidence receipt,
and kernel-validated protocol.

### 4. Evidence precedes a finding

A finding must traverse to the exact target revision, hypothesis, triggering input,
content-addressed artifact, execution environment, observed effect, verifier identity, and
scope decision. A hash proves byte identity; independent reproduction establishes the
security claim.

### 5. Negative outcomes remain first-class

`blocked`, `invalid`, `null`, `inconclusive`, `failed`, `not_reproduced`, and
`out_of_scope` are never collapsed into success, zero, or silence. Corrections and
retractions retain the same visibility as positive results.

### 6. Canonical state is reconstructible

An append-only, canonical event stream is the durable mission record. Every projection,
search index, dashboard, cache, and vector store is disposable and rebuildable. Closing a
mission closes its append authority.

### 7. Untrusted work is contained

Target bytes, papers, websites, datasets, model output, generated code, tool output, and
persistent memory are untrusted input. Candidate execution occurs only in a proved
hard-isolation profile with no ambient credentials and default-deny egress.

### 8. Learning cannot seize authority

MINERVA may propose strategy changes from retained evidence. Promotion is offline,
versioned, benchmarked against positive and negative fixtures, human-reviewable, reversible,
and unable to modify authority policy, evaluators, benchmark labels, or production bytes
directly.

### 9. Claims are bounded

Etzio uses `implemented`, `modeled`, `proposed`, `blocked`, and dated measured results
precisely. “State of the art,” “safe,” “autonomous,” “solved,” and “production-ready”
require a named benchmark, comparator, scope, date, and retained evidence.

### 10. Every gate proves refusal

Every consequential control has a known-bad fixture showing that it rejects an invalid
case. A happy-path demo is not evidence that a gate exists.

## Engineering doctrine

- Keep one target-neutral kernel and add capability through versioned domain and technique
  packs.
- Build the smallest dependency-complete vertical slice before adding parallelism.
- Separate policy authority, scientific adjudication, and generated intelligence.
- Prefer deterministic protocols and content-bound identities over conversational state.
- Preserve raw evidence privately and expose only the minimum necessary projection.
- Measure precision, recall, coverage, stability, cost, and time; never optimize one metric
  by hiding another.

## Estate boundary

Etzio is independent from Odeya, Sentinel, Aweb, Maestro, Telos, Inbar, and every other
project in Daniel Wahnich’s estate. It may study reusable engineering patterns. It does not
import their runtime code, stores, identities, credentials, routes, or control authority.
